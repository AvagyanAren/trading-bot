Simple close-to-close returns. Pearson correlation and conditional means/probs at fixed lags 1,2,3,6,12 bars. Multi-bar returns use overlapping shifts. Overlapping lag-1 of a k-bar return is mechanically large because windows share k-1 bars; interpret only non-overlapping lags (3-bar at lag 3, 6-bar at lag 6, 12-bar at lag 12). n is large; statistical significance is ignored unless the magnitude could matter after ~0.30R costs. No AR model fitting.
