2. What appears predictable
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Expansion persistence. Prior 12-bar normalized range quartiles have 12h
  future_vol 1.75%, 2.31%, 2.53%, 3.05% (monotonic). High current range
  predicts a larger future high-low window.
- Barrier resolution rate. High-vol and weekday sessions hit +/-1% more
  often. That raises both P(+1R first) and P(-1R first). It is not a
  directional coin.
- 2024 bull drift in median close-to-close return, growing with horizon
  (~0.002% at 15m to ~0.16% at 24h). Drift is slow relative to 1% noise.
- Weekend compression. Saturday MFE/MAE and future_vol are about half of
  weekdays. 1% barriers are rarely reached.
- A small bounce after short-term declines, especially when EMA20<=EMA50.
  Median 12h lift is about +0.05% to +0.08% versus the matched baseline.
  Recovery to the pre-move close is common (often minutes to a couple of
  hours) because many "declines" are tiny.

6. Time-scale findings
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At 15m-4h, most paths never touch 1%. The rare touches are slightly more
often -1R first. That is hostile to a tight LONG stop.

Median close-to-close return is positive at every horizon and rises with
time, as expected in 2024. The 12h median is 0.0955% and the 24h median
is 0.1641%. Both are well below one 0.30% round trip.

EMA20>EMA50 has negative median forward return from 15m through 4h
(-0.012% at 1h). EMA20<=EMA50 has a positive short-horizon median
(bounce) but a worse 1R race (more -1R-first). Trend-following LONG at
5m therefore faces: fade against the EMA in the median, and a left tail
that still hits a 1% stop.

There is no horizon at which a simple LONG 1% barrier becomes plausible.

7. Trend findings
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Binary EMA20>EMA50 vs EMA20<=EMA50:

- Slightly better resolved 1R coin in the uptrend (52.8% vs 49.6%).
- Worse median 12h forward return in the uptrend (0.067% vs 0.124%).
- Short-horizon median is negative in the uptrend and positive in the
  downtrend.

That is not "trend helps LONG." It is closer to "the 5m path mean-reverts
a little while the 1R race is a coin." Spread Q4 (strongest uptrend) has
the highest raw race (+5.0pp) and 11/12 months above baseline, but median
forward return is not better than ALL, and the quartile path is not
monotonic (Q2 is the worst race). Mechanical A on spread_Q4 is rejected.

8. Momentum findings
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Continuation is rejected.

Positive vs negative prior 3-bar return: 12h race 42.43% vs 42.41%.
Quartiles are U-shaped: Q1 and Q4 have higher MFE/MAE and higher raw
barrier-hit rates because extremes are volatile. Q4 (strongest prior
up-move) has a worse 12h median forward return than Q1 (strongest prior
down-move) at 12-bar and 24-bar lookbacks.

That is the opposite of momentum continuation on the median, and it is
not a clean monotonic race.

9. Mean-reversion findings
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the only directional hypothesis that is not rejected outright.

After the most negative 3/6/12-bar quartile, 12h median forward return
is about +0.05% to +0.07% versus ALL, and a bit more versus the same
bearish-EMA baseline (ret12 Q1 ∩ bear: median +0.063% vs bear, race
+3.6pp, 11/12 months). Time to recover the pre-move close is short
(median 10 bars after a 3-bar drop among Q1).

It is still far below 0.30R. The 1R race remains ~50% resolved. Q4
(strong rally) also expands, so the quartile picture is U-shaped, not
a pure "buy dips" curve. Grade B: real, small, not strategy-grade.

15. Multiple-testing warnings
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Twelve investigations, six hour buckets, seven weekdays, four lookbacks,
four quartiles, trend splits, and eight horizons were pre-specified, but
the family is still large.

Rejected as discoveries: Monday's raw race (activity, not coin), spread
Q4 (non-monotonic quartiles, no median payoff), 08-12 UTC (+2.9pp, mixed
months, thinning aliases with a 12h grid so some hour buckets have zero
thinned rows), any single U-shaped extreme quartile.

Global every-144-bar thinning is a dependence diagnostic, not a way to
improve results. For 4-hour UTC buckets it aliases with the 12-hour grid
and is not informative; monthly splits are the robustness check there.

  future range than Q1 on 2025, no direction claim).

  is dominated by clustering and tiny moves.
- Cost reduction. The 0.30R stack, not the 51% coin, is what kills 1:1
  barrier LONG. That is an implementation question, not a 5m edge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CRITICAL QUESTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After all 12 investigations, do we have evidence that BTCUSDT 5m contains a
repeatable directional edge that can plausibly support a simple LONG-only
trading strategy?

ANSWER: NO

WHY:
The resolved +/-1R coin is about 51% at 12h and 24h in a bull year. A 51%
1:1 barrier minus ~0.30R costs has expectancy near -0.27R, which is what
strategy A and the random baseline both deliver per trade. Apparent bucket
winners (Monday, high vol, strong EMA spread) mostly change how often 1%
is touched, not the coin. The only genuine directional hint — a weak bounce
after short-term declines — is +0.05% to +0.08% of median 12h return, an
order of magnitude below cost, and it sits in a large multiple-testing
family. That cannot plausibly support a simple LONG-only 5m strategy.

If not, what type of market property appears more promising than directional
prediction?

ANSWER: volatility prediction / expansion persistence (current 12-bar range
and weekend-vs-weekday compression). Regime detection of "is 1% likely to
be reached" is more supported than "will +1% arrive before -1%." Event-driven
or longer-timeframe work is more promising than another 5m LONG filter.
There is no clear directional 5m edge on this dataset.
